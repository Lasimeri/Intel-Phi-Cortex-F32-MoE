# The host orchestrator (host/orchestra.h)

User direction (2026-10-26 06:22): "Use the host CPU's FMA instructions in order to write a LLM orchestration
model/tool in order to help keep the output of tokens generated by the Phi coherent and lingually correct." Then
(12:21): "fix the word filter".

The orchestrator sits between the card's logits and the sampler in host/cortex.c (`--orchestra NGC`) and runs on the
Ryzen 7 5800X's AVX2 + FMA3 units (the host has no AVX-512). Nothing in it comes from model output: the n-gram counts
come from the human token stream (tools/ngcount.c), the word list from the human text (the coherence tool of the F64
tree), and the repetition constraint reads only the sample being generated.

## Per token

1. `log p_phi(v) = logits(v) - logsumexp(logits)`: the max by an AVX2 compare pass, `exp` by a degree-6 FMA
   polynomial with the exponent assembled in the integer field, the sum by horizontal adds.
2. `log p_ng(v | p2, p1)`: Dirichlet-smoothed backoff trigram -> bigram -> unigram over the count model (unigram
   counts and hashed bigram and trigram contexts, each with its top-8 next tokens). The entries outside the two top-8
   lists share one closed form (`(alpha / d3) (alpha / d2) p_uni`, one vector add on the log unigram), the listed
   entries are computed exactly. Prior mass ORCH_ALPHA 8 per backoff step.
3. The mixture `(1 - lambda) log p_phi + lambda log p_ng + logsumexp(logits)`, one fused multiply-add per lane. The
   log-sum-exp is added back so the result keeps the offset of the card's logits: the sampler's repetition penalty
   divides positive values by 1.2 and multiplies negative ones, so a mixture without the offset would change what the
   penalty does. At lambda 0 the logits are unchanged (the identity gate below).
4. The lingual filter over the whole vocabulary. The letters of the word being written are tracked across pieces
   (lower case, apostrophes inside words kept). A piece is valid when the letters it adds keep the word on a listed
   word or a proper prefix of one, and every word it closes is listed. Every bad piece goes to
   `min(value - ORCH_PENALTY, best valid - ORCH_MARGIN)` (7 and 16 nats), so the sampler chooses among the valid
   continuations with the model's own preferences between them, and a word committed as a prefix is completed to a
   listed word. Words count for the filter from corpus count ORCH_WORD_MIN (50) on (`--word-min`); the scorer keeps
   the complete list. Cost: a piece that closes the word (3,039 of the 4,096, the end-of-text piece among them) has
   a constant verdict computed once; a letter-led piece is judged only when its first letter can follow the partial
   word (27 lookups per step decide that).
5. The repetition constraint: a candidate (within ORCH_WINDOW 10 nats of the top) that completes a token 4-gram
   already present in this sample loses ORCH_REPEAT 5 nats (an open-addressed set of 65,536 slots).

The sampler then runs unchanged (temp 0.5, top-k 75, top-p 0.95, min-p 0.05, repetition penalty 1.2 over the last
64 tokens, llama.cpp's order).

## Artefacts found on the way, each caught by a measurement

- v1 (results/orchestra-1.log): `log` on all 4096 entries per step and a scalar top-64 scan, 459 us per token; the
  mixture looped on frequent phrases (rep4 0.09 to 0.12). v2 added the closed-form backoff, the candidate window and
  the 4-gram block: 342 us.
- v2 returned log-probabilities without the log-sum-exp offset (results/orchestra-2.log): every value negative, the
  sampler's penalty multiplied repeated tokens' values by 1.2 instead of dividing, the samples looped and shortened
  their sentences (8.5 to 9.1 words against 10.3 to 10.5). The identity gate (lambda 0, no filter, no block must
  reproduce the plain text byte for byte) catches this class.
- The first word filter judged only the window's candidates and penalised the bad ones alike: a window whose
  candidates were all bad sent the broken word out ("thes" of "these"). Its repair, a 2-nat clamp below the best
  valid continuation, leaked through the repetition penalty (results/orchestra-4-mix.log, tools/misses.c: 22 broken
  words of 323 with the filter alone on the mixed model); the mix word list's count >= 3 entries include transcript
  junk ("ap" 32, "ud" 39) whose prefixes open junk paths ("udainilate"); and the end-of-text piece, exempt, closed
  the 4 residual broken words of results/orchestra-5-*.log. Hence v3: every piece judged, a 16-nat margin, count >= 50,
  EOT a closing piece.

Reading: the filter alone makes every word a listed word on every model (its purpose), and on a piece-spelling
model it raises hit5 from 0.63 to 1.00; the mixture is what makes the word pairs human (big 0.98 to 1.00 against
0.90 to 0.95 with the filter alone); the block keeps rep4 inside the band, and the filter alone can raise it (stories
seed 1: 0.061). The card's time per token (360 to 396 us) is not changed by any of this; the host's grows from 323 to
about 400 us, so a sampled token costs about 0.75 ms end to end. Meaning is not scored by these metrics.

## Artefacts

- data/stories-10m.ngc: tools/ngcount over the first 10M TinyStories training tokens (469,778,456 B).
- data/mix/mix-50m.ngc: the same over data/mix/train.u16 (4,024 bigram and 937,714 trigram contexts).
- data/wordlist-stories.txt, data/wordlist-mix.txt: words and pairs with counts (`coherence build`, count >= 3).

## Limits

- The constraint strengths (7, 16 and 5 nats, window 10 nats, prior 8, count 50) were set once from the measured
  failures, not searched.
- A listed junk word with count >= 50 passes the filter ("u" 2,459 in the mix list).
- The count model and the word list are per corpus; a model trained on the mix is served with the mix files.
- The filter forbids words outside the corpus, proper nouns included: by design, the model is kept to the human
  vocabulary it was trained on.
